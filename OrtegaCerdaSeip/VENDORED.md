# Vendored from MR1682065

These files are copied verbatim, unmodified, from the companion formalization of
J. Ortega-Cerdà and K. Seip, *Multipliers for entire functions and an interpolation problem of
Beurling*, J. Funct. Anal. 162 (1999), 400–415 ([OS99] in the paper),
<https://github.com/joaquimortega/MR1682065>, commit `05e3ac2ca83f8862636e1dd002467ad9add83396`.

They are the import closure of `OrtegaCerdaSeip.Lemma3.Main` (Lemma 3 of [OS99], the multiplier
lemma `OCS.lemma3`, used in the proof of Corollary 2 and of Theorem 2 (ii)) and of the
`OrtegaCerdaSeip.Bernstein.*` files (Bernstein space, Phragmén–Lindelöf, normal families, sinc).
All of them are free of `sorry`. Do not edit them; put adaptations in `FourierFrames/`.
